## Overall interpretation

Lekka’s most credible differentiator is Local Radar: privacy-safe, location-aware local discovery combined with social and business utility. The highest-risk gap is evidence quality: the repository is broad, but critical user, security, device, and release paths remain unverified. The next milestone should be validation and release hygiene rather than another uncontrolled feature sprint.
